# What You Build

Every week ends with something that runs, is tested, and is defended live. The weekly builds add up to a project each month, and in Month 3 those projects grow into one big build aimed at your chosen path.

## Month 1: an AI engineer's stack, built from your own parts

| Week | Build | By the end you can |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tok`: a BPE tokenizer, samplers, and a JSON-constrained decoder, on TestPyPI | Explain what a token costs, choose a decoding method, and guarantee valid structured output |
| 2 | A context and memory library for long-running assistants | Fit the right context in a token budget and keep facts correct across months |
| 3 | `minisearch`: an inverted index, BM25, HNSW and hybrid retrieval with reranking | Show with numbers how often retrieval finds the right document, and why |
| 4 | An agentic harness: the loop, tools, skills, MCP, using your Week 1–3 parts | **Month 1 project:** an agent built on code you wrote and can defend, at the Day 30 idea review |

## Months 2 and 3

Month 2 opens the box: neural nets and autograd, attention and a GPT, vision and multimodal models, and generative models. Month 3 is core ML engineering (optimization, training at scale, inference, post-training) and one big build for your path. The weekly pages are released in order in the [guide](https://dpaul0501.github.io/ai-engineer-100-labs/).

What the big build becomes depends on your [primary path](paths.md):

| Path | The big build becomes |
|---|---|
| **Open source** | A released package with outside users, or a substantial feature merged upstream |
| **Academic papers** | The experiments behind a workshop paper, or a provisional patent |
| **Hackathons** | Hackathon entries, each extending the build |
| **Consultancy** | A paid engagement for a client, written up as case studies |
| **Product** | A launched product with users and paying customers |
