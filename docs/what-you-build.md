# What You Build

Every week ends with something that runs, is tested, and is defended live. The weekly builds add up to a project each month, and in Month 3 those projects grow into one big build aimed at your chosen path.

## Month 1: a tested AI tool for one real problem

| Week | Build | By the end you can |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A pocket search CLI over a folder of documents, with an LLM answer on top | Build a tested search tool from scratch, explain its design with numbers, and defend every line |
| 2 | An extraction tool that turns messy text into validated JSON, scored against a hand-labeled eval set | Say how accurate your LLM tool is, with an error bar |
| 3 | A scalar autograd engine with gradient checks, exposed to an LLM as a tool | Prove your gradients are right |
| 4 | A model trained on your own problem's data, behind a REST API with a cache, called by an agent loop you wrote without a framework | **Month 1 project:** the four builds wired into one tested AI tool, presented at the Day 30 idea review |

## Month 2: a deployed system for your problem area

| Week | Build | By the end you can |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | A leak-checked boosting model on real tabular data, stored in SQL, with an agent that remembers earlier sessions | Prove your model does not leak |
| 6 | RAG over your problem area, with a hybrid retriever, a small reranker, and a retrieval eval set, containerized and tested in CI | Show with numbers how often retrieval finds the right chunk |
| 7 | A BPE tokenizer trained on your own corpus, and masked multi-head attention written from scratch that matches PyTorch | Explain every merge and every line, and measure how well your LLM judge agrees with you |
| 8 | A small GPT trained from scratch, a multi-agent tool exposed over MCP, and a full design review | **Month 2 project:** RAG, agent, and model with evals, deployed and defended at the Day 60 idea review |

## Month 3: one big build for your path

About 70% of each day goes to this build, grown from your Month 1 and Month 2 projects.

| Week | Sprint |
|---|---|
| 9 | A reviewed spec for the big build, its foundations running with CI and an eval harness |
| 10 | Sprint 1: the first must-have features, and a measured answer to "does fine-tuning beat prompting here?" |
| 11 | Sprint 2: every claim of "better" backed by a confidence interval, and your first outside validation |
| 12 | Sprint 3: production-ready, deployed with a rollback, monitored, within a spend cap, and used by real outside people |
| 13 | The Day 90 idea review, and your path's main document written and reviewed |
| 14 | Polish, a dress rehearsal, and **demo day on Day 100** |

What the big build becomes depends on your [primary path](paths.md):

| Path | The big build becomes |
|---|---|
| **Open source** | A released package with outside users, or a substantial feature merged upstream |
| **Academic papers** | The experiments behind a workshop paper, or a provisional patent |
| **Hackathons** | Hackathon entries, each extending the build |
| **Consultancy** | A paid engagement for a client, written up as case studies |
| **Product** | A launched product with users and paying customers |
