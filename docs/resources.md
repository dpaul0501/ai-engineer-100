# Resources

## Companion books and courses

- [From LeetCode to Learning Systems](https://mldsa.github.io/) pairs every algorithm with its ML use case. It is the reading companion for the DSA track:
    - [Hashing](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/part1/chapter01-hashing/) (Week 1)
    - [Top-K](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/part1/chapter02-topk/) (Weeks 1 and 6)
    - [Dynamic programming](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/part1/chapter03-dp/) (Weeks 9 and 10)
    - [RAG](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/ml-spine/chapter-rag/), [LLM internals](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/ml-spine/chapter-llm/), and [agents](https://mldsa.github.io/docs/ml-spine/chapter-agents/) (Weeks 6–12)
- [LLM Foundations from Scratch](https://dpaul0501.github.io/llm-agent-reads/) builds a Transformer from tokens to generation. It is the core text for the ML/DL track in Weeks 7–10.
